Quick calm tool

Breathe In...

How are you feeling right now?

Choose what fits — we’ll show a simple next step.

For anxiety (2 minutes)

  1. Take 3 slow breaths (long exhale).
  2. Name 5 things you can see.
  3. Relax shoulders + jaw.
  4. Pick one small next action (water, walk, message someone).

If this keeps happening often, talking to a trusted person or professional can help.

For overwhelm (reset)

  1. Write down the top 3 things on your mind.
  2. Circle the one you can do next.
  3. Break it into a 5-minute step.

You don’t have to solve everything today — just move one step.

For low mood (gentle start)

  1. Stand up and stretch for 20 seconds.
  2. Get sunlight or fresh air if possible.
  3. Do a “tiny win” (make bed, quick shower, reply to one message).

Low days happen. Tiny wins still count.

Learning mode

Mental health is about how we think, feel, and connect — and how we respond to stress. Building resilience is like training a skill: small practice, over time.

The Basics

Mental health includes our emotional, psychological, and social well-being.

Emotional

How you feel and cope with feelings like stress, anger, sadness, and joy.

Psychological

How you think, focus, solve problems, and respond to challenges.

Social

How you connect with others, set boundaries, and feel supported.

A reminder

“Mental health is not a destination, but a process. It’s about how you drive, not where you’re going.”
